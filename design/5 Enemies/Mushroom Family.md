# Mushroom Family

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · family per [[Enemy Design Standards]] · updated 2026-07-07

**Silhouette language**: capped stalks — a hat readable in any crowd; drift-shuffle root-step locomotion (slowest family). **Palette**: pale stalk, cap in family-toxin colors, gill-glow underneath (bioluminescence law, [[Art Direction]]). The mushroom family owns AREA — they make the arena itself hostile and force melee players to move. Ian roster (verbatim): small, poison, armored, ancient.

## Mushgloom — the drifting puff `Wave 4+`

- **Role**: slow area pressure; crowd filler with a hat. Answered by: anything — but its spore puff punishes lazy positioning
- **Stats** `TUNE`: HP 35 · speed 25% · mass 1.5 · essence 2
- **Attack**: proximity spore puff (26f cap-squeeze windup, 60 px cloud, 5 dmg + brief slow). Cap visibly inflates first
- **Reactions**: full menu; launches leave a spore-trail arc (pretty AND readable)
- Reference: MUSHGLOOM named in v2 pack

## Toxicap — poison-on-death `Wave 6+`

- **Role**: area denial; makes kill placement matter. Answered by: killing it where you WON'T stand, hammer ring from range, tongue-pull to a corner
- **Stats** `TUNE`: HP 40 · speed 25% · mass 1.5 · essence 3
- **On death**: 80 px poison cloud, 12 s linger `TUNE`, 2 dmg/s + poison stack. Cloud is honest — dense sickly green with drifting motes ([[VFX Standards]]), enemies immune
- Poison-build players eventually WANT Toxicap deaths near enemies (poison → ignite combo fuel, [[Co-op Design]]) — the enemy becomes a tool at high skill, peak design
- Overkill law: burst-killing does NOT prevent the cloud (unlike Bogpop) — consistency lesson: sludge rewards burst, mushrooms punish position

## Shellshroom — the armored cap `Wave 8+`

- **Role**: the armor gate; breaks auto-pilot arcs. Answered by: hammer (armor ×2), spear flank, tongue-expose, bowling another enemy into it
- **Stats** `TUNE`: HP 110 · speed 20% · mass 2.5 · essence 6
- **Armor**: the cap is a 240° frontal shield — armored absorb feedback (gray flash, dust, *clink*). The soft gill-glowing back is the tell AND the target. It slowly rotates to face its attacker (rotation speed is the tuning dial for how hard flanking is; in co-op it simply cannot face both — designed teamwork)
- **Attack**: cap-first ram lunge (30f windup), knocks frogs back (mass shove, low damage)
- **Launch clause**: a launched enemy colliding with a Shellshroom flips it (turtle rule) — 2 s belly-up full vulnerability. The bowling reward, on purpose

## Eldershroom — the ancient buffer `Wave 16+`

- **Role**: priority target that reshapes a wave. Answered by: focused dive through its escort (spear lunge, tongue-grapple in), or killing the escort to strand it
- **Stats** `TUNE`: HP 200 · speed 15% · mass 5.0 · poise: no flinch below heavy · essence 12
- **Aura**: 240 px spore ring — enemies inside get +20% speed & damage, visible as gill-light underlighting them `TUNE`. The aura is the THREAT; the body is a slow sad giant
- **On death**: aura collapses inward — buffed enemies briefly STUNNED (the tide-turn moment; big audio payoff)
- Ancient = tallest silhouette in the family, moss-bearded, face like a sleeping elder — dreamlike-and-dangerous tone anchor ([[Art Direction]])

## Mushroom Elite — family champion `Elite waves`

Shellshroom frame, obsidian cap, ember gill-glow. **One trick**: its ram leaves a burning spore trail (burn status introduces fire pressure from the enemy side). Turtle rule still applies — elites keep their family's counterplay.
