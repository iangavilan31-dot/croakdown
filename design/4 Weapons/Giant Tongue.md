# Giant Tongue

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · universal secondary + build path · updated 2026-07-07

The frog IS the weapon — the tongue is the verb that makes it true. Always available on every frog regardless of weapon, on a short cooldown; also a full primary-weapon build path (Tongue build, Lightning Tongue end-state).

## Universal secondary (every frog, always)

- **Input**: Tongue button ([[Movement and Controls]]), aimed like attacks
- **Behavior**: elastic ray, 320 px reach `TUNE`, 0.22 s out-and-back, cooldown 2.5 s `TUNE`
  - Hits **light/mid enemy** (mass ≤ 2.5): pulls it to melee range, briefly stunned (0.3 s) — the pull-into-swing setup is the game's signature one-two
  - Hits **heavy enemy/prop/boss**: pulls the FROG to it (grapple-hop, i-frame-less travel) — aggressive gap close
  - Hits **world**: interacts (glowing fish, golden dragonfly, shrine offerings — [[Secrets and Discoveries]]); grabs distant essence clumps
- **Feel**: elastic SFX (stretch → snap), tongue sprite is thick and meaty with a wet impact star ([[VFX Standards]]); pulled enemies squash on arrival
- Never damages by default — it's a *positioning* verb. Damage comes from what you do next

## Evolution ladder `LOCKED (names)` — via shop evolution cards

| Tier | Name | New property |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tongue** | Universal behavior above |
| 2 | **Piercing Tongue** | Skewers through the first target to pull TWO lights (or pins a light to a heavy behind it) |
| 3 | **Forked Tongue** | Two prongs, 30° spread — pulls two separate enemies into one swing window; cooldown −0.5 s |
| 4 | **LIGHTNING TONGUE** | Pulls arc chain-lightning between tongue targets and anything they touch on arrival; pulled enemies arrive *stunned and conductive* (next hit chains) |

Tongue-build items (shop) add: tongue damage, cooldown reduction, auto-lash on dash, tongue length — turning the secondary into a primary is a legitimate build ([[Progression and Evolution]] build variety: Tongue, Lightning Tongue).

## Co-op currency

The tongue is the teamwork verb: cross-pulls into a partner's [[Bog Hammer]] windup, yanking a Shellshroom's flank open for a partner's [[Reed Spear]], saving a downed partner by pulling an exploder away. Synergy verbs pay golden spore bursts ([[Co-op Design]]).
