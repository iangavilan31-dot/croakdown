# Weapon Roadmap (Phase 3 content lines)

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · design briefs, NOT specs — each gets a full page meeting [[Weapon Design Standards]] before build · updated 2026-07-07

Ian's remaining roster (VISION.md verbatim: Thorn Whip, Dragonfly Glaive, Poison Cane, Frog Fists) plus reference-pack sword variants as skins/sub-tiers. None enter Phase 3 without the completeness contract.

## Thorn Whip
Long-range crescent lash; pulls LIGHT enemies slightly toward the frog on hit (mini-tongue on every swing). Role: mid-range crowd control, bleed application (thorns = Ian's blood pillar). Risk to watch: overlaps tongue identity — differentiate as *damage-first with pull garnish* vs tongue's *pull-first*.

## Dragonfly Glaive
Twin-bladed staff, fast figure-eight arcs, brief hover-glide after dash (dragonfly wings shimmer). Role: mobile skirmisher, the "movement build" weapon; attacks while dashing at tier 3+. The flashiest animation budget in the roster — earns its place as the style weapon.

## Poison Cane
A gnarled druid's cane dripping toxin. Role: status engine — modest direct damage, strong poison stacking, tier 4 turns overkill corpses into spore clouds. The build-around for Poison end-state and the poison → ignite co-op combo ([[Co-op Design]]).

## Frog Fists
No weapon at all — wrapped fists, brawler chains, uppercut launcher, belly-flop slam. Role: the joke that's secretly top-tier; shortest reach, highest attack speed, launch-heavy. Evolution ends at **THOUSAND-YEAR PALM** (croak shockwave). Pure charisma pick; animation-led design.

## Reference-pack sword variants
The v2 pack names 8 swords (Lily Blade, Reed Katana, Moss Cleaver, Bone Slicer, Glow Lotus, Toxic Bane, Wisp Edge…). These map to the [[Sword Line]] as tier skins and shop rarities (palette-swap law, `docs/GOAL_PAPERDOLL.md`), NOT as separate lines — quality over quantity holds.

## Selection rule for what builds next
After v1, the next weapon is chosen by (1) which build end-state in [[Progression and Evolution]] lacks a weapon anchor, (2) praise-mining demand ([[Design Philosophy]]), (3) animation budget reality ([[Animation Standards]]). Never "because it's next on the list."
