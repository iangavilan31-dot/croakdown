# Mother Mosquito — wave 10

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · boss per [[Boss Design Standards]] · updated 2026-07-07

**The legend**: the matriarch of every whine in the night swamp — a cathedral of chitin and stolen blood. The midpoint exam: sustained pressure, mobility, and the game's most literal use of the blood pillar. Where the [[Drowned King]] stands and trades, Mother flies, drains, and HEALS — the first boss you can lose slowly.

- **Silhouette**: 280 px wingspan `TUNE`, six needle-legs folded like altar candles, abdomen a translucent reliquary that VISIBLY fills with blood as she drains — her health story told in her body, no UI
- **Tag line**: `MOTHER MOSQUITO — she gave you life, give it back`
- **Arrival**: the whine arrives 5 s before she does (audio-first, rising from every speaker); fireflies flee; she unfolds from the canopy dark above the arena edge
- **Arena state**: night deepens one grade; her wing-drone shimmers standing water into ripple-static

## Kit

| Attack | Anticipation | Behavior | Punish window |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Needle Descent** | 30f — she rears, needle-legs spread, shadow shrinks under target | Dive-bombs a frog along a shimmer-line (Darter grammar, king-sized) | 45f stuck proboscis-first in the mud if she misses — THE window |
| **The Tithe** | 35f — hovers low, proboscis glows | Latches the nearest frog: drains 4 HP/s **into her own HP** (visible red thread + abdomen filling). Broken by damage threshold or partner tongue/hit | Post-break stagger 40f |
| **Brood Chorus** | egg-scatter croon | Scatters 4 eggs (1 s glyphs) → Skeeters | The eggs (one-hit kills, pop satisfyingly) |
| **Dust Communion** | wing-shudder | Sheds a Duskmoth-style occlusion veil around herself — you fight her silhouette by eye-glow and audio inside it | Veil drifts off her after 4 s |

## Phases (HP 2600 solo `TUNE`, ×1.7 2P)

1. **Vespers** (100–65%): Needle Descent + Brood Chorus rhythm
2. **The Tithe** (65–30%): drain unlocked — the fight's identity turns: interrupt-the-heal becomes the core job. Solo: damage threshold breaks it (burst matters). 2P: **she only Tithes while the OTHER frog is far** — forced togetherness mechanic
3. **Communion** (30%→ or 90 s in phase 2): permanent thin veil, Descents chain ×3, abdomen glows through fog (her own blood-hoard betrays her position — poetic counterplay)

## Kill

Slow-mo: the final blow ruptures the reliquary abdomen — every drop she stole RETURNS as a fountain of heal-motes + essence (the game refunds the whole fight's drain: mercy as spectacle). Her wings detach and drift down as two giant permanence decals. The whine stops — the silence is the trophy.

## Design notes

- Blood pillar, literalized: drain visible in her body, refunded at kill. No numbers anywhere
- She counters tanky-passive builds (drain scales with proximity uptime) and rewards the tongue (drain-break = the tongue's hero moment at wave 10, exactly when Tongue builds come online — [[Progression and Evolution]])
- The whine is an audio-design deliverable with a dedicated mix lane ([[Audio Direction]]): players should *hate-remember* it, like the VS Reaper bell
