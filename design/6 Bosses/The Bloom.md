# The Bloom — wave 20, the finale

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · boss per [[Boss Design Standards]] · updated 2026-07-07

**The legend**: the swamp's own heart, flowered wrong. All run long, lotus imagery has meant reward — the Bloom Chest, the combo lotus, evolution petals. The finale weaponizes the game's most trusted symbol: a colossal corrupted lotus that IS wave 20. The final exam: everything — telegraphs, adds, occlusion, arena control, co-op — composed into one multi-phase spectacle.

- **Silhouette**: a 512 px `TUNE` lotus colossus rooted at arena center; petals like cathedral doors, each veined with hot-pink corruption (the danger accent at maximum, [[Art Direction]]); a radiant core glimpsed between petal seams
- **Tag line**: `THE BLOOM — the swamp dreamed too deep`
- **Arrival**: wave 20 has no spawn pulses. The breather light never lifts; instead every glow in the arena (spores, shrines, lanterns) DRAINS in streams toward center; the ground swells; the Bloom unfolds from beneath — the arena was its bud all along
- **Arena state**: per-phase corruption spread — mud veins glow pink, water darkens to ink

## Structure — a siege in four movements (HP 6000 solo `TUNE`, ×1.7 2P)

### I. The Sepals (100–75%)
Six petal-shields orbit-fold around the core (each 300 HP `TUNE`, independently destructible — skull-bulb vine bar shows petals as its notches). The Bloom attacks with **root tentacles** (glyph → eruption lines) and **spore volleys** (lobbed AoE puffs, Mushgloom grammar at siege scale). Petals regrow after 25 s if not all broken `TUNE` — sustained pressure, split targets in 2P.

### II. The Lure (75–45%)
Core exposed, petals splay flat as terrain. **Light-lure beams**: the core sweeps radiant beams that *heal enemies and hypnotize* — a frog caught in a beam walks toward the Bloom (mash/partner-hit to break; solo gets a longer mash window). Spawns Emberfly swarms (light that lies, now systemic). The Bloom sings — the soundtrack's melody, detuned ([[Audio Direction]] leitmotif corruption).

### III. The Blizzard (45–15%)
Spore blizzard fills the arena edges (occlusion ring — the fighting space is a lit island around the boss); root tentacles chain-erupt; a **Sludge King and an Insect Queen** rise TOGETHER as corrupted heralds (the run's elite lessons return, corrupted-pink). Deadline-as-boss: the blizzard ring slowly contracts on a soft timer — the arena itself becomes the enrage.

### IV. The Opening (15%→)
The Bloom OPENS fully — the radiant core naked, beautiful, firing everything at once but taking double damage. Music strips to a heartbeat + the pure leitmotif. Both frogs, everything they've built, 60 seconds of maximum spectacle at maximum readability (this phase is [[Quality Gates]] exhibit A: if it's illegible, we failed feat 5).

## Kill

The final blow at 0.3× slow-mo: the core CRACKS like the danger-pip skull; light pours out; the corruption drains backward along every mud-vein; petals bleach from pink to white as they fall — becoming the victory screen's ground. The true Great Lotus blooms small and clean where the colossus stood. Fireflies return in their hundreds. **The victory tableau is shot on the run's accumulated battlefield** — corpses, blood, the Leviathan's ridge, twenty waves of story ([[Vision]]).

## Design notes

- No new grammar in the finale — every mechanic is a wave-1–19 lesson at mythic scale (composition, not novelty: the finale honors the player's education)
- 2P: beams lure both frogs in opposite directions during phase II (the run's last "come save me")
- The Bloom kills the old TD Heartbloom irony gracefully: the "great glowing lotus at arena center" from the dead design returns as the final BOSS. The pivot, made canon
