# Drowned King — wave 5

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · boss per [[Boss Design Standards]] · updated 2026-07-07

**The legend**: the first frog king, who loved his crown so much he wore it into the deep. What rose again is bloated, regal, and wrong — a drowned colossus of the old court. The tutorial legend: teaches telegraph literacy, punish windows, and add triage — the exam for everything waves 1–4 taught.

- **Silhouette**: a vast waterlogged bullfrog, 320 px `TUNE`, split-open crown of roots and rusted gold fused to the skull; robes of pond-scum; one eye milk-white, one glowing. Grotesque-regal, zero humanoid
- **Tag line** (intro card): `DROWNED KING — the crown remembers`
- **Arrival**: the deep pool at arena center boils; the crown breaks the surface first; he ROARS a wall of spray (cosmetic push, no damage — pure spectacle)
- **Arena state**: water level rises +1 tile ring around the pool; drowned-gold light from below

## Kit

| Attack | Anticipation | Behavior | Punish window |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Royal Tongue** | 35f — throat swells, gold glow | A king-sized mirror of the player's own tongue: lashes a line, drags a frog 200 px toward him | Tongue retract 50f — his mouth hangs open |
| **Wave of Court** | 40f — raises both arms, water rises with them | 360° water ring expands from his body (hop over it — it's slow; dash i-frames trivialize it, teaching dash-as-defense) | 30f after the ring passes |
| **Belly of State** | 45f — inhales, inflates hugely | Forward belly-slam, 140 px shock ring + mud crater | LONG — 70f grounded, gills heaving. THE damage window |
| **Summon the Court** | horn-croak, 3 glyphs | 3 Drowned Fawns (pale Blobbit-variants with tiny antlers, palette-legal) waddle from the pool | The fawns themselves — bowling ammo into the king |

## Phases (HP 1400 solo `TUNE`, ×1.7 in 2P)

1. **The Court Convenes** (100–60%): kit as above, stately pace — a rhythm to learn
2. **The Crown Slips** (60%→): crown CRACKS (phase bulb shatters on the vine-bar); he weeps black water; Royal Tongue becomes a double-lash; Wave of Court gains a second offset ring; battle damage accumulates (robe tears, exposed ribs)
3. *(2P only)* **Two Subjects**: Royal Tongue targets BOTH frogs simultaneously — dragged together, tangled (dash breaks free / partner hit severs) — the first co-op rescue story

## Kill

Final blow at slow-mo: the crown falls off, rolls, and sinks; the king deflates with a long sad croak into the pool; drowned gold erupts as essence. The pool stays gold-lit for the rest of the run (permanence law). Bloom Chest rises where the crown sank.

## Design notes

- Wave-5 placement: early enough to promise "this game HAS bosses," gentle enough to lose only to ignored telegraphs
- Royal Tongue mirroring the player's verb is the design thesis: bosses speak the game's own language back at you
- Fawns arrive during Belly recovery on a fixed script (phase 1) — teaching add-triage inside a rhythm, not on top of chaos
