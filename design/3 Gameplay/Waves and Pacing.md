# Waves and Pacing

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · updated 2026-07-07

20 waves, 25–35 minutes, four swamp legends. Structure carries the research-verified Brotato grammar; all TD framing (Heartbloom, build phases, node forecasts) is dead.

## Wave structure

- **Wave N duration**: spawn budget ≈ 25 s + 5 s/wave, capped at 70 s (waves 10–19); wave 20 finale ≈ 100 s `TUNE`. Wave ends when its spawns are dead
- **Breather between waves**: untimed. Essence tally → [[Shop and Economy|shop]] → both frogs hold Ready ([[Movement and Controls]]) → dusk-to-night light shift + music rise announce the next wave — **world-state change, never a banner** (MUST-BEAT feat)
- **Spawn telegraphs**: every spawn = ground glyph + smoke-cloud emergence (~1 s, reference pack SPAWN anim). During the breather, glowing eyes mass at the arena edges showing next wave's *composition* — world-space forecast, re-homed from the TD forecast glyphs
- **Wave 20 exception**: [[The Bloom]] IS the wave

## Stronger vs weirder (the law)

- **Stronger** = linear stat ramp per wave (HP, count, essence). **Enemy speed never scales**

## Bosses

Every few waves the music dies, the water stills, and a legend arrives. Ritual and rules: [[Boss Design Standards]]. Elites (4/8/12/16) are family champions — bigger palette-shifted variants with ONE new trick, not damage sponges.

## The Bloom Chest (the one authored ceremony)

Boss kill → a colossal lotus rises where the boss died and OPENS: full pause · petals unfurl one by one · slot-machine relic reveal · essence fountain ticks up · music surge · both frogs pulled into frame. This is the single most-polished moment in the game (MUST-BEAT feat: the VS-chest jackpot). Relic goes to the *team* ([[Progression and Evolution]]).

## Difficulty

**Danger pips 0–3** at run start: +12 / 26 / 40% enemy HP & damage, extra elite waves at max `TUNE`. Hidden 4th pip: [[Secrets and Discoveries]]. Solo scaling: −25–30% enemy HP/count off the 2P baseline (tune for 2P first — research law, [[Co-op Design]]).

## Pacing texture inside a wave

Spawn budget releases in 3–5 pulses, not a faucet: pulse → carnage → 4–8 s of collection/repositioning → pulse. Quiet beats are where the swamp breathes ([[Environment and Reactivity]]) and where players talk shop. The wave director may spend up to 20% of budget on an "ambush" pulse behind the players — always telegraphed by eyes + audio sting from that edge `TUNE`.
