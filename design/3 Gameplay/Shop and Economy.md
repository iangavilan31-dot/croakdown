# Shop and Economy

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · updated 2026-07-07

One currency, one shared wallet, one-glance decisions. Grammar is proven Brotato patterns, re-skinned to the swamp and rebuilt for co-op.

## Essence (the single currency) `LOCKED`

- Essence = XP AND gold (Brotato materials trick). Drops from every kill; bonus from overkills, wall-splats, and combo tiers ([[Game Feel Standards]])
- Small magnet radius (grows with Pickup stat); the body pays for the economy — you hop to wealth, you don't vacuum it
- Drop rate decays ~1.5%/wave to a 50% floor `TUNE` (inflation guard)
- Visual: glowing spore-motes, gold. Never text popups — collection reads as light entering the frog

## Level-ups

Essence fills the level ring; on level: hard-pause, **3 stat cards**, pick one. Co-op: simultaneous split panels, each player picks their own ([[Co-op Design]]). Stat list: [[Progression and Evolution]].

## The shop (after every wave)

- Hard-paused, untimed, **shared screen — one shop, one wallet, two buyers** (Ian: "players constantly discuss purchases")
- **4 cards** per roll: weapons · items · relic-less (relics are ceremony-only) · **evolution cards** when prerequisites met ([[Progression and Evolution]])
- **One-glance grammar** (MUST-BEAT feat): icon + tier color (white/blue/purple/amber) + price + green/red stat deltas. Decision < 2 s per card. No paragraphs — flavor lives on opt-in hover
- **Lock** toggle per card (persists to next shop) · **Reroll** cost `⌊W×0.75⌋` then `+⌊0.4×W⌋` per roll `TUNE`
- Tier gates at shops 2/4/8 `TUNE`; shops 1–2 are training wheels (no trap purchases early)
- **Co-op etiquette in systems**: purchases debit the shared wallet with both players' consent implicit — but **rare+ cards flash a "both ready" pulse**: second player can veto within 1.5 s (taps = contest → alternating first-pick rule applies, Brotato co-op precedent) `TUNE feel, keep frictionless`
- Shopkeeper: a lantern-frog merchant with idle animation, reacts to purchases (croak of approval) — menus contain ambient motion too ([[Design Pillars]])

## What's for sale (design targets)

Items are designed backward from the twelve build end-states in [[Progression and Evolution]] — every item should push toward at least one end-state and combine with at least two others. Item icon language: reference pack pickup set (swamp heart, glow seed, moss armor, spirit orb, toxic vial, bone shard, lotus petal, frog charm, mushroom cap, sludge core, dried tadpole, golden egg, cursed relic…).

## UI law

Shop and level-up are the ONLY text-dense surfaces in the game, and both are hard-paused (Brotato law). Panels slide in on the side opposite the frogs (MUST-BEAT feat 9). Chrome: dark swamp-glass, cream text, gold accents ([[Art Direction]]).
