# Secrets and Discoveries

> Part of the [[00 START HERE|CROAKDOWN bible]] · updated 2026-07-07

Ian law: **the game hides things worth discovering.** Secrets are small, real, and never required — they reward curiosity, reward attention to the living swamp, and give friends things to tell each other. Ian's seed list (VISION.md): hidden frog statues, golden dragonflies, mushroom circles, ancient shrines, ghost frogs, glowing fish, secret caves.

## v1 secrets (specced, buildable)

| Secret | Trigger | Payoff | Design note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Golden Dragonfly** | Rarely (~1/4 runs) crosses the arena mid-wave, fast | Tongue-catch → essence fountain + a unique relic-tier buff | The tongue-skill flex moment; visible to both players, catchable by one |
| **Mushroom Circle** | A faint ring of tiny mushrooms spawns in the breather; stand inside 4 s | Ring blooms → random wild buff for the wave (giant hops, gold tongue, spore trail) | Rewards exploring the arena instead of shop-camping |
| **Frog Statue** | Mossy statue among arena props; hit it 3× (it rings like stone) | Croak blessing: heal + brief gold rim-light | Statues also foreshadow lore (ancient frog wardens) |
| **Ghost Frog** | Stand still 8 s in deep fog | A pale frog appears, follows at distance; blocks the next lethal hit then departs | The "did you see that?!" co-op story generator |
| **Glowing Fish** | Flickering light under deep water; tongue it | Full heal + Fisher's Charm (pickup radius) | Teaches: the tongue works on the WORLD, not just enemies |
| **Ancient Shrine** | Rune stone glows after an elite dies near it; offer 50 essence (hold) | Curse-or-gift roll (never strictly bad — "cursed" = powerful with a visible drawback) | The gambling ritual; shared-wallet argument fuel |
| **Hidden 4th danger pip** | Hover/hold the 3rd pip 5 s at run start — the skull cracks open | Danger 4: twin-finale, elite density, unique title-screen tint afterward | Carried from the TD era; pure menu mischief |

## Rules for secrets

- Never text-explained. Discovery = noticing → experimenting → payoff. The world hints visually (glint, flicker, hum) per [[Environment and Reactivity]]
- Every secret has a distinct audio signature ([[Audio Direction]]) — sharp ears find them first
- Secrets log as silent discoveries (icon appears on the run-end canvas) — collection pressure without checklists
- Post-v1 pool (secret caves, statue lore chain, boss-specific secrets) lives in [[Roadmap]] Phase 3; praise-mining passes may add more ([[Design Philosophy]])
